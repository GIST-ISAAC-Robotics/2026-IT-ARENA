        Description

        The ESCON2 Compact 60/12 is a dynamic, ready-to-connect 4-quadrant PWM servo controller. Suitable for the efficient control of brushed DC motors and brushless EC motors (BLDC) with Hall sensors and/or encoders up to 720 watts/1,440 watts.
