Motor Controllers - 7A to 300A

Intelligent, programmable motor controllers built for robotics, automation, and demanding industrial systems. RoboClaw delivers high-precision closed-loop DC motor control with quadrature encoder feedback, integrated PID with auto-tuning, and flexible interfaces including USB, Serial, RC, and Analog.

With libraries for Arduino, Raspberry Pi, ESP32, and ROS 2, integration takes minutes. Configure and optimize any RoboClaw controller using Motion Studio. From 7A to 300A in single- and dual-channel models, RoboClaw delivers precise motion control across a wide range of power levels.
