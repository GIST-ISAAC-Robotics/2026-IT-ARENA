 1. Product overview YDLIDAR T-mini Plus is a 360 degrees 2D LiDAR (hereinafter referred to as T-mini Plus)
developed by EAI team. Based on the principle of ToF, it is equipped with related optics, electricity,
and algorithm design to achieve high-precision laser distance measurement, while measuring the
distance, the mechanical structure rotates 360 degrees to continuously obtain angle information,
thereby realizing 360 degrees scanning distance measurement and outputting point cloud data of the
scanning environment. 2. Product Features ➢ 360 degrees scanning and ranging, 6-12Hz scan frequency  ➢ Ranging frequency up to 4000Hz  ➢ High accuracy, stable performance  ➢ Strong ability to resist ambient light interference  ➢ Class I eye safety standard 3. Applications ➢  Robot navigation and obstacle avoidance  ➢ Robot ROS teaching and research  ➢ Regional security  ➢ Environmental scanning and 3D reconstruction  ➢ Navigation and obstacle avoidance of home service robots/ robot vacuum cleaners 4.
