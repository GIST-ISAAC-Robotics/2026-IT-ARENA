 High Performance Lidar 
 MS200 lidar features a rotating optical scanning system and integrates custom SPAD chip. With its compact size and high performance, it enables fast and accurate scanning measurements within a 360°/12m range. It is widely used in various fields including robot vacuum, service robots, security, and scanning. 
 dTOF Ranging Introduction 
 dTOF (Direct Time of Flight) refers to a single-line laser lidarutilizing direct time of flight distance measurement technology. It calculates distance directly based on the difference in pulse transmission and reception times, ensuring consistent measurement accuracy regardless of distance. This capability enables precise detection of environmental information, even at longer distances. 
 High-performance SPAD Chip 
 Customized single-photon chip optimizes measurement performance from the ground up. 
 Light and Compact Design 
 Compact structural design to free robots from design limitations. 
 12m Scanning Radius 
 Provide a 50% increase in sensing range compared to typical triangulation lidar, effectively ensuring the robot's navigation and mapping capabilities. 
 40000 Lux Anti-glare 
 Effectively avoid strong light interference, enabling measurement and mapping in complex lighting environments and , which reduces the difficulty of robot algorithm design. 
 Adjustable Scanning Frequency 
 MS200 lidar comes with a driver for motor speed control. You can adjust the scanning frequency through software to accommodate different scenes and robots. 
 High Sampling Rate, High Resolution 
 With a 360° omnidirectional scan and a high sampling rate of 4500Hz, MS200 achieves a minimum angular resolution of 0.4°. This allows for enhanced environmental perception and precise mapping. 
 CLASS 1 Safety 
 MS200 uses a low-power infrared laser emitter as its light source and is driven by modulated pulses. The laser emitter can emit light within an extremely short time frame. It has been certified under CLASS 1 eye safety for the protection of children and pets' eyes. 
 Strong Compatibility 
 Support ROS1 and ROS2, and compatible with various controllers such as Raspberry Pi and JETSON, making it suitable for various robot scenarios. 
 Lidar Mapping and Navigation 
 Point-to-point Navigation & Multi-point Navigation 
 Lidar Guard 
 Lidar Tracking 
 Provide ROS Robot Mapping and Navigation App 
 Achieve mapping, navigation, image viewing, and other functions through the app. (Android users only). 
 Debugging Software 
 Provider debugging software which support windows system.
