    Introduction


    This STL-19P LiDAR sensor offers a ranging distance of up to 12m and 360° environmental scanning, which can realize autonomous mapping, and obstacle detection.



    The sensor is compact and light, and its size nearly equals to a ping-pong ball and the weight nearly to 2 AA dry batteries.



    Also, it can resist the strong light of 60 Klux, which achieves high-precision distance measurement indoors and outdoors.


    The provided SDK works well with both ROS1 and ROS2 systems.


    And the adopted brushless motor boasts long-lasting operation with high-speed data transmission.
